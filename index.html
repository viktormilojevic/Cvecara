    Order by 2pm

    Choose an arrangement or tell us a budget and let our florists interpret it.

From the studio

We buy the flower before we know the order

Every Monday our head florist walks the growers' rows and buys what's actually ready — not what a catalogue says should be in season. The week's collection above is built backwards from that walk, which is why it changes so often. It also means a rose bought this week was likely still in bud on Friday.
